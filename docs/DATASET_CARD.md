# Dataset card

## Identity and provenance

- Dataset name:
- Source owner:
- Retrieval method and date:
- License or access agreement:
- Immutable version, checksum, or query:

## Observation and sampling

- What does one row represent?
- What population or process produced the rows?
- Which units repeat, interact, or depend on one another?
- Which time, site, batch, subject, or spatial boundaries matter?

## Schema and measurement

Document each identifier, feature, unit, missing-value convention, target, and
availability time. Identify post-outcome variables and other leakage risks.

## Data access and privacy

State what may be committed, what remains local or remote, which credentials
are required, and how CI obtains a safe structural fixture.

## Representativeness and limitations

Describe known exclusions, selection effects, label limitations, measurement
changes, and populations for which the dataset is not evidence.
